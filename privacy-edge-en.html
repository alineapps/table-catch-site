Privacy Policy (Microsoft Edge)

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how ALINE APPS ("we") handles information in the Microsoft Edge extension "Table Catch" (the "Extension").

1. Table contents and the pages you visit

The Extension reads tables only on the tab where you click the toolbar icon, use the right-click menu or press the keyboard shortcut. All conversion and file creation happen inside your browser. We never send table contents, the URLs of the pages you visit, or your browsing history to us or to any third party.

2. Information stored in your browser

InformationWherePurpose
Export settings (format, merged-cell handling, etc.)storage.syncTo keep your settings. If Microsoft Edge sync is on, they are shared with your other computers through your Microsoft account.
Pro license key and activation detailsstorage.localTo enable Pro features
Free-trial count and datestorage.localTo count the daily free tries

This information is not sent to our servers. Removing the Extension deletes the information in storage.local.

3. Information sent outside your browser (license checks)

Only when you activate, check (every 7 days) or deactivate a Pro license key, the Extension sends the following to the License API of our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com):

  • Your license key
  • An activation name ("edge-" followed by 8 random characters; it contains no personal information)

See the Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy for how Lemon Squeezy handles this information.

4. Purchases

Purchases and payments for Pro are handled by Lemon Squeezy. You enter your name, email address and payment details with Lemon Squeezy; we do not receive your card or other payment details. We may receive order information (such as your email address and what you purchased) from Lemon Squeezy to confirm orders and answer your questions.

5. Analytics and advertising

The Extension does not use analytics, advertising, or usage tracking.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties except where required by law.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
