Export any web table to Excel & CSV in one click.

Merged cells, numbers and dates come out clean, ready to calculate with.

What it does

  • Finds the tables on the page (including inside iframes) and exports the ones you pick.
  • Use the right-click menu or Alt+Shift+E to click a table on the page and export it.
  • Formats: Excel (.xlsx), CSV (UTF-8 or Shift_JIS), TSV, JSON, Markdown and HTML.
  • Turns 1,234, 12.5%, (500) and dates into real numbers and dates, including Japanese notation such as ¥1,200, △500 and 2026年11月10日. Codes with leading zeros and phone numbers stay as text.
  • Your data stays in your browser. Table contents are never sent anywhere.
